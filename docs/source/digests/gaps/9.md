### 2.2 Resilience values (exact)

|---|---|---|

| Upstream throttle | "One concurrency and rate limit per upstream, shared across every copy of its service" | G61:198 |
| Backpressure | "a saturated downstream answers at once (`limit_exceeded`, or 503 with `Retry-After`)" | G61:124 |
| Concurrency limit refusal | "`limit_exceeded` (429), or `503` with `Retry-After` when a service sheds load" | G62:124 |
| In-flight (edge) | "One limit per replica; a stream counts by connection; over it, `unavailable` with `Retry-After`" | G67:111 |

| Service crash | ATLAS-GATEWAY-006: "A service that crashes makes its capabilities `unavailable` within five seconds, and nothing else." | G61:56 |
| Codes | `unavailable` 503, retryable, "The service is unhealthy (its circuit is open); `retry_after`"; `limit_exceeded` 429, retryable; `upstream_failed` 502, retryable; `deadline_exceeded` 504, not retryable; `budget_exceeded` 403, not retryable; `internal` 500 | D93:102, :98, :101, :103, :85, :100 |
